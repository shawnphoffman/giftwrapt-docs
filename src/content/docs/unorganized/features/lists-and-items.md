---
title: Lists and items
description: How wish lists work in GiftWrapt — items, photos, prices, priorities, and collaborators.
---

A list is a collection of items the list owner wants. Each item carries:

- **Title and notes** — what it is, sizing, color preferences, anything that helps a giftgiver
- **Photo** — uploaded directly or pulled in by the [URL scraper](/features/url-scraping/)
- **Price** — pasted manually or scraped from the source URL
- **Priority** — so giftgivers can see what matters most
- **Quantity** — for items where multiples make sense (socks, books)
- **Comments** — back-and-forth between owner and giftgivers

Lists default to private. Sharing happens via [partner and guardian relationships](/features/guardians-and-partners/), or by adding **list editors** for collaborative lists (e.g. a couple's joint Christmas list).

Giftgivers can also suggest items via **add-ons** — useful when you spot something the recipient should have on their list but didn't add themselves.
