If your admin has turned on **Gift Suggestions**, a **Need Ideas?** button appears with the filters at the top of someone else's list. With the setting off, which is the default, the button is not there and nothing else changes.

## How It Works

Press **Need Ideas?** and a dialog asks two optional questions: your budget and the occasion. Press **Find Ideas** and you get two kinds of answer.

### From Their List

The best three things still open on the list, most wanted first. With a budget, anything priced over it is left out.

- It only offers things you could claim right now. Fully claimed and unavailable items are skipped, a "pick one" group is skipped once someone has claimed from it, and in an "in order" group only the next item is offered.
- Each one says why: its priority, whether it fits your budget, and how many are left.
- Choose one to close the dialog and jump to that item on the list.

This part is plain ranking, not AI. Nothing is sent anywhere to produce it.

### Not on Their List

New gift ideas that are *not* on the person's list, based on what their lists say about their taste.

- Each idea says what to look for (the kind of thing, useful features, sensible variations), why it suits them based on their list, and a rough price band. There are no product links and no search button: the idea is the starting point, and where you shop is up to you.
- **Save Idea** adds it, with what to look for, to your private gift ideas for that person, creating that list the first time. From there it shows under **Gift Ideas** on their lists and can be turned into an off-list gift once you buy it. The person never sees your gift ideas.
- Ideas you do not save are not kept anywhere.
- **Change Budget** takes you back to the questions.

<Aside type="caution" title="Check Before You Buy">
The ideas come from an AI model. It does not know what is in stock or what things cost today, and it never supplies product links or points you at a store. Treat an idea as something to research, not a recommendation to buy a particular product.
</Aside>

### What the AI is shown

To suggest something sensible, the model is shown the person's first name, the items on their lists that you can already see (title, price, priority, and category), whether each item is already claimed, your own gift ideas and past gifts for them, and your budget if you gave one.

It is never shown who claimed anything, what anyone paid, claim notes, item notes or links, anything you cannot see yourself, or last names, email addresses, and birth dates. The full, generated list is on the [AI Provider](/configuration/ai/#what-each-feature-sends) page.

### Who can use it

- An admin turns it on under **Admin > AI** (**Gift Suggestions**). It is off by default.
- Child accounts get the picks from the list but not the AI ideas. An adult can ask for ideas for a child's list.
- It never works on your own list: it is for shopping for someone else.
- You can ask up to ten times an hour.

## What Their List Is Mostly About

When [Suggestions](/features/suggestions/) is on and enough of a list has been analysed, the dialog also opens with a one-line summary such as "Their list is mostly Sports & Outdoors, Clothing". It comes from categories GiftWrapt already worked out for the items; nothing new is sent anywhere to show it.
