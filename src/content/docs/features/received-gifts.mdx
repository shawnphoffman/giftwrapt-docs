The **Received gifts** page is where the list owner sees what they actually got: the items their giftgivers claimed, who claimed each one, and any add-ons giftgivers volunteered along the way. Until the reveal happens, this page is empty for an item.

## How an Item Gets Here

Items show up on Received gifts when both conditions are true:

<Steps>

1. Someone has **claimed** the item (created a `giftedItems` row).
2. The item has been **archived** (its `isArchived` flag is set to true).

</Steps>

Archive is the visibility trigger. Until archive, the list owner sees a normal list - no badges, no claimer names, no spoilers. After archive, the claim data becomes visible.

Two things can flip the archive:

- **You reveal the list manually**, from the archive badge next to the list title or from **Archive all purchases** in list settings. This is the "I've already opened these gifts, show me who gave them" action, and it covers the whole list at once rather than one item at a time.
- **The auto-archive cron** flips it on a schedule per list type. See [Claims and reveal](/features/claims/#auto-reveal) for the per-type defaults; admins tune the offsets at `archiveDaysAfter*`.

<Aside type="note" title="Gifters Can Never Reverse the Reveal">
Once an item is archived, you've seen the claim data, and no giftgiver can put it back - the reveal is recipient-driven by design. The archive flag itself isn't one-way: someone with edit access to the list (you, a [guardian](/permissions/guardians/), a list editor) can set it back to unarchived, though today only the [iOS app](/features/ios-app/) exposes a control for that. Either way it just tidies the page; it doesn't unsee anything.
</Aside>

## What Shows Up on Each Entry

| Field | Source |
|---|---|
| **Item** | Title, photo, price, your original notes. |
| **Gifter** | The user who claimed it (or first claimer for split claims). Renders avatar + name. |
| **Co-gifters** | Other users listed in the claim's `additionalGifterIds`. Two gifters from the same household collapse into a single pair. |
| **Quantity** | For items where multiple people claimed partial quantities, each claim is its own row. |

If your [partner](/permissions/partners/) was a co-gifter or claimed it, they're credited too - that's how partner-shared gift credit works on the receiving side as well.

<Aside type="note" title="What a Gift Cost Stays on the Gifter's Side">
The page tells you who, not how much. A gifter can record a total cost and private notes on their claim, but this page never reads either field, so neither reaches you - not at the reveal, not afterwards. The one exception is an add-on, which carries its own cost (see below).
</Aside>

## List Add-Ons

Below your items, the Received gifts page surfaces **list add-ons**: extra gifts giftgivers volunteered that weren't on your original list. An add-on is a much simpler row than an item, and it shows:

- **A description.** One free-text field, and it's required - there's no separate title, photo, or product URL on an add-on.
- **The gifter**, rendered as a household the same way claims are.
- **A cost**, if the gifter recorded one.

Add-ons never appear on your own view of the list before the reveal, though the giftgivers can see each other's all along. You can decide whether to add an add-on to a future list, send a thank-you for it, or just acknowledge it - GiftWrapt doesn't enforce a workflow.

<Aside type="caution" title="No 'Mark as Received' on Add-Ons">
There was briefly a gifter-facing "mark as given" action on add-ons; it was removed in 2026-04. The recipient is the only one who decides when a gift counts as received.
</Aside>

## Dependents

When a list has a [dependent](/permissions/dependents/) as its subject, the Received gifts view for that list's guardian still shows everything correctly attributed - the dependent's identity renders as the recipient, the guardian's identity renders nowhere (they're the list manager, not the recipient).

A dependent never sees a Received gifts page of their own (they don't have an account). Their guardians see one per dependent they manage.

## Why No "Received-At" Timestamp?

There's no dedicated reveal timestamp on items. The Received gifts query uses `items.updatedAt` as a proxy for "archived at," which works because the archive write is the most recent change for an archived item.

If your deployment ever needs an exact reveal time (audit, accounting), treat that as a new column - don't overload `updatedAt`.

## Privacy

The recipient can always see claim data for items they've revealed. That's the whole point. But the page never exposes things the recipient shouldn't see:

- **Other people's restricted-filtered claims** stay hidden (Received gifts is owner-facing, where the [Restricted](/permissions/privacy/) filter doesn't apply, but the principle holds for any future cross-user view).
- **Pending unclaimed items** don't show up here. The page only renders archived items with at least one claim.
