## Why?

GiftWrapt started as a labor of love for my family in early 2024. v2 is a ground-up rewrite of that app, shaped by everything v1 taught me and built around simplicity, ease of use, and privacy. Now, it is self-hostable and ready for your friends & family.

## What's in the box

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Lists & items" icon="list-format">
		Public and private lists for different occassions. URLs, photos, prices, priorities, quantities, and more for every item. Collaborative list editors for joint lists.
	</Card>
	<Card title="No spoiled suprises" icon="approve-check">
		Mark items as claimed, visible to other giftgivers but not to the recipient. Gifters are revealed to the recipient through configurable schedule or manually.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Guardians, dependents, and partners" icon="heart">
		Real household relationships. Guardians can help manage children's lists; fully manage dependents' lists for pets, babies, and more. Set your partner for joint credit when it matters.
	</Card>
	<Card title="URL scraping" icon="external">
		Paste a product link and the app pulls title, photo, and price. AI extractor falls back when the basic scrape comes up empty.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Self-hostable" icon="rocket">
		Drop-in Docker compose with Postgres + Garage S3, or deploy to Vercel, Railway, Render, or Coolify in one click.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Bring your own storage" icon="cloud-download">
		Garage and RustFS bundled for self-host. Recipes for AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, and Supabase Storage.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Optional email" icon="email">
		Resend integration for comment notifications and birthday reminders. Skip it entirely and the app still works fine.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Multi-theme" icon="moon">
		Supports both light and dark mode. Picks up your system preference by default.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
