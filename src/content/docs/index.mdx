## What's in the box

Built for the kind of household with a couple of adults coordinating gifts for each other and the kids, plus a relative who keeps emailing Etsy links.

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Lists & items" icon="list-format">
		Photos, prices, priorities, quantities, notes, comments, and add-ons. Collaborative list editors for joint lists.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Claims, hidden from the owner" icon="approve-check">
		"I've got this" markers visible to other giftgivers but not to the recipient. Reveal flow opens it up after the gift is given.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Guardians & partners" icon="heart">
		Real household relationships. Guardians manage kids' lists; partners coordinate with each other.
	</Card>
	<Card title="URL scraping" icon="external">
		Paste a product link and the app pulls title, photo, and price. AI extractor falls back when the basic scrape comes up empty.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Self-hostable" icon="rocket">
		Drop-in Docker compose with Postgres + Garage S3, or deploy to Vercel, Railway, Render, or Coolify in one click.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Bring your own storage" icon="cloud-download">
		Garage and RustFS bundled for self-host. Recipes for AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, and Supabase Storage.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Optional email" icon="email">
		Resend integration for comment notifications and birthday reminders. Skip it entirely and the app still works fine.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Multi-theme" icon="moon">
		Light, dark, and a Christmas mode. Picks up your system preference by default.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
