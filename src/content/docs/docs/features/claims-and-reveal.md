---
title: Claims and reveal
description: How giftgivers coordinate without spoiling the surprise.
---

Two giftgivers shouldn't buy the same dishwasher. GiftWrapt solves this with **claims** that are invisible to the list owner.

## Claims

When a giftgiver opens a list they can mark any item as **"I've got this"**. Other giftgivers see the claim immediately and know to pick something else. The list owner sees a normal list — no claim indicators, no spoilers.

Claims are reversible. If plans change, the giftgiver can release the claim and the item goes back into the unclaimed pool.

## Reveal

After the gift has been given, the list owner can trigger the **reveal flow** to see who claimed what. This is opt-in and one-directional — the owner asks to see, the giftgivers don't have to choose to share.

The reveal also surfaces add-ons (items giftgivers suggested) so the owner can decide whether to add them to a future list.
