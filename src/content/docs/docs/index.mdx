GiftWrapt is a self-hostable wish list app for families and small groups. Make a list, share it with the people who shop for you, and let them quietly claim items so nobody buys the same thing twice. You own your data and your deploy.

## Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Lists & items" icon="list-format">
		Photos, prices, priorities, quantities, notes, list editors for collaboration, comments on items, and add-on suggestions from giftgivers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Claims & reveal" icon="approve-check">
		Giftgivers mark items as "I've got this" without the recipient seeing. A reveal flow uncovers who got what after the fact.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Guardians & partners" icon="user-group">
		Guardians get full access to their kids' lists. Partners see each other's public lists. Real relationships, not just shared logins.
	</Card>
	<Card title="URL scraping" icon="external">
		Paste a product URL and the app pulls title, photo, and price. AI extraction kicks in for stubborn sites. <a href="/docs/scraping/">Pipeline details &rarr;</a>
	</Card>
	<Card title="Themes & email" icon="seti:happenings">
		Light, dark, and Christmas modes. Optional Resend integration for comment notifications and birthday reminders.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Admin tools" icon="setting">
		User management, scraping/AI tuning, storage inspection, and email tests. <a href="/docs/guides/local-dev-admin/">Recovery guide &rarr;</a>
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
